**Why is there only one day of transactions?**  
`http 200`, `complete`, as analyst

> # Banking reporting periods and data coverage
>
> Transactions cover a single business day, 2026-03-15. This is why daily, weekly and monthly transaction trends, period-over-period transaction comparisons and transaction forecasts cannot be produced. The transaction timestamp in the source file has times within that one day, and the silver layer stores only the date.
>
> Account openings run from 2010-01-02 to 2026-06-06. Customer onboarding runs from 2010-01-01 to 2025-11-18. Card issues run from 2018-01-01 to 2023-06-24. Loan disbursements run from 2024-01-01 to 2025-02-04. Loan instalments run from 2024-01-31 to 2026-03-15 by due date. [Source: banking/reporting_periods v1]
>
> # Banking data dictionary
>
> Banking analytics read the catalog banking and schema gold in Unity Catalog. The approved business tables are gold_transactions, gold_accounts, gold_branches, gold_cards, gold_customers, gold_loans and gold_loan_repayments, with daily and monthly aggregates named agg_*.
>
> gold_transactions holds one row per transaction: amount in INR, channel (ATM, NETBANKING, IMPS, POS, UPI, NEFT), merchant_category (CASH, FOOD, ELECTRONICS, TRAVEL, UTILITIES, GROCERY, SHOPPING, FUEL, JEWELLERY), status (SUCCESS, FAILED, PENDING), txn_type (DEBIT, CREDIT, TRANSFER), city, and txn_timestamp, which is stored as a date with no time of day. [Source: banking/data_dictionary v1]
>
> # Banking data access policy
>
> Analytics answers are aggregates. Account numbers, card numbers, customer identifiers, transaction identifiers, names, phone numbers, email addresses, PAN, Aadhaar and addresses are not returned to business users, and approved queries use identifiers only to link tables.
>
> CVV and card security codes are never available to anyone, including administrators. Card expiry dates are never returned in full.
>
> Sessions are limited to one business domain, so a banking session cannot read insurance, retail or healthcare data, and asking for it is refused rather than answered from banking data.
>
> Forecasts for business users are released only after an administrator approves them. Approval cannot override a privacy or access restriction. [Source: banking/access_policy v1]
